# Security & Trust

> _Work in progress._

Tarmhack'AI is built **local-first**. This page will explain, in plain terms, how the app
treats your machine and your data — so you can trust a tool that touches sensitive AI
configuration files.

Planned sections:

- **Local-first by design** — what runs on your machine and what (does not) leave it.
- **No telemetry** — what we do and don't collect.
- **Filesystem safety** — how writes and deletions are contained to expected locations.
- **Signed releases** — how to verify the integrity of what you install.
- **Reporting a vulnerability** — how to reach us responsibly.
